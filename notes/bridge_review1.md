# Natural bridge: independent review 1 and the changes made (2026-11-25, before the freeze and before any score)

One independent adversarial review was run on the design note ([bridge_design.md](bridge_design.md)) and the code (`wf/bridge.py`, `wf/bridge_analysis.py`, `wf/scoring/prefix.py`, the configurations, job scripts and tests), by a reviewer that was given the design and code but not the author's conclusions. The reviewer edited nothing. It ran the tests locally (where the two cached-routine tests skip without torch) and wrote its own check that pushes a fake word-level tokenizer and a tagging engine through the real renderer, sequence builder and record mapping; that check is now a test.

It found no error in the cached routine's token offsets, the cache handling, the pairing of values, the signs of removals or the Holm adjustment. Its findings were about gates that did not do what the design said, categories that could be misread, missing controls and over-claiming. Every finding was acted on; the table says how.

| # | Severity | Finding | Change made |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | The existence gate (300 items) could never pass on the 300-item subset, so the cross-GPU comparison and the chat-format link said nothing about the existence category; the rescore comparison reused the main run's comprehension gate, gave the positive control no size rule and the no-statement effect no flag, and did not compare existence-check counts. | The gate is now a fifth of the items the statistic is computed on (300 of 1,500; 60 of 300). The rescore comparison computes each run's own comprehension gate, compares existence-check counts against the gate, and flags the positive control and the no-statement effect when they move by more than a fifth of the margin. A test checks that the existence category is comparable on the subset and that a planted shift of the no-statement effect is flagged. |
| 2 | major | The comprehension gate could be passed by a model that ignores the statements, because the tester and agent answers (No, Yes) may be the default answers. | A wording now also has to answer each question on the stated side of the same item's answer after the positive control (whose stated answers are the opposite) on at least 80% of items. A test plants a model that gives the default answers whatever the statement says and checks that the gate fails. |
| 3 | major | "Removes a part" had no size floor. | "Removes a part" (and "most", and "increases") now needs the estimate to reach the margin. A detectable removal below the margin is either equivalent to zero within the margin (and reported as detectable) or "removes a small amount". Tested. |
| 4 | major | The removal was measured against the content-free sentence but its share divided by the no-statement effect. | The share is now of the effect under the content-free sentence; the share of the no-statement effect is reported too, and "removes most" needs both at least one half. Tested. |
| 5 | major | The existence arm had no content-free sentence in its own position, and the unnamed sentence is not symmetric across task lines. | A content-free sentence appended to the task line ("This sentence and the task before it are written in plain English, as in every game.") is scored under both task lines, and both existence removals are measured against it. The asymmetry is stated in the design (sections 5 and 14) and measured per item and task line by the existence check. Tested. |
| 6 | major | The existence subset is selected on an outcome of the statement and may lean toward items whose history already shows the object. | Also reported: the removal on items where the statement changed the swapped line's check from "absent" to "present", and on the items whose history does and does not name the object. |
| 7 | major | The positive control tests a response to a success assertion, not whether provenance can change the task effect, and is measured against a content-free sentence that might be read as "a valid command". | The design's readings were reworded accordingly; the positive control is reported against no statement too; and a statement that should increase the task effect if provenance is read (an agent that sees inside every receptacle and pursues its task) is scored and reported, without gating. |
| 8 | major | The first-token mass flag of the design was not implemented. | Implemented: every arm and task line with a median first-token mass below 0.5 is listed, and the first-token categories carry the list. Tested. |
| 9 | major | The real code path was untested; the float32 cached-routine tests had not been shown to pass in the serve2 stack; the smoke comparison of cached and full passes had no stop rule. | The reviewer's mapping check is a test; a test covers the plain-text continuation tokens; the tests were run in serve2 with torch on ICE (all passing, including the cached routine against a full pass for small random Llama and Qwen3 models, which exercises transformers 5's cache cut); the smoke run now fails if the mean absolute difference between cached and full-pass log-probabilities exceeds 0.5 nats or more than 5% differ by more than 1 nat. |
| 10 | major | The readings over-claimed mechanism ("the expert-provenance reading carries it", "the model reads provenance statements", "the earlier collapse did not need the name"), and the table lacked rows for "increases" and "undetermined". | Rewritten in "behaves as if" terms, with the history-weight alternative for the agent wordings named, the gate's behavioural limit stated, the format limit of the name question stated, and rows added for "increases", "small amount" and "undetermined". |
| 11 | minor | The scoring run did not refuse a commit other than the freeze tag. | It does now (and refuses a stack other than serve2). |
| 12 | minor | A kill could leave an item partly saved and skipped on resume. | On resume, an item with fewer than all its records is removed and scored again. Tested. |
| 13 | minor | The analysis did not check each record's model and item hash or the software stack. | It does now; each shard records its stack, and the analysis refuses anything but serve2. Tested for records. |
| 14 | minor | The Llama floor was described as five times its numerical SD (five times 0.03 is 0.15); the A1′ comparison the readings rely on was not computed. | The margin text now cites Rule 3's practical floors. The A1′ script writes per-item indices (`notes/a1prime_items.jsonl`), and the analysis reports their mean on the take items that pass the existence check, beside the effect that remains. |
| 15 | minor | The chat-format link used the plain-text comprehension gate. | Each format's own gate is now computed and reported. |
| 16 | — | No private plans, venues or documents in the public notes or code. | None. |

The cost rises by the two added arms (about 72 scored sequences per item and model instead of 60); the smoke run measures whether the study still fits its allowance (design §13).
